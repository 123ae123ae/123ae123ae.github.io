Privacy Policy

Pulum is a manual bookkeeping app for individuals and shared households.

Pre-release draft: operator identity, providers and retention rules require final review.

Operator and contact

Pulum is provided by an individual independent developer operating as Uzum Studio. Contact: uzumstudio.dev@gmail.com.

Data and purposes

Email is required for login. We also process your display name, ledger memberships, entered transactions, account balances, categories, budgets, recurring rules, notes and necessary login and email logs. These support bookkeeping, sharing, sync and export; security logs prevent abuse and help resolve faults. The legal bases are providing the requested service and legitimate interests in service security. We do not connect directly to banks, sell data or use advertising tracking.

Sharing and providers

Invited members access shared ledgers according to their roles. Supabase provides authentication and the database in France; GitHub Pages and Brevo are planned for public pages and transactional email. Providers process only data needed for their services; email is not used to market ledger content. Necessary technical data may be processed outside the EEA; providers and transfer safeguards must be verified before publication.

Retention and deletion

Data remains while the account or ledger is used. Deleting records or ledgers may initially be a logical deletion. Delete your account in Settings → Account information; shared-ledger owners must first transfer or delete their ledger. Sole-owned ledgers are cleaned up with the account; shared records still used by other members remain after a member leaves. Security logs and backups follow necessary security purposes and provider retention cycles.

Device storage and security

Devices store sessions, language preferences, offline records and pending drafts. Exports and other offline devices may retain copies. We use encrypted transport and access controls, not end-to-end ledger encryption.

Rights and updates

Contact us to request access, correction, export, deletion or restriction, or to object or withdraw consent where applicable. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the competent supervisory authority. Material changes will update this policy with notice where necessary.
