Service

Pulum provides manual bookkeeping and shared ledgers, not banking or payment services. Check entries against actual balances. A ledger's currency is fixed at creation, without automatic conversion.

Your responsibilities

Protect credentials, enter only authorised data and check invitations and sharing permissions. Do not access others' ledgers or abuse the service. Export regularly and protect your files.
